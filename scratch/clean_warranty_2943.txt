### Globe Type Valve
Inclusions:
1 Defects in materials or workmanship in body, plug, seat, and stem.

2 Leakage within ANSI/FCI Class standards (e.g., Class IV or V).

3 Actuator function (manual or automatic) within rated service.

4 Internal trim operation under specified pressure and temperature conditions..
Exclusions:
1 Trim wear due to cavitation or flashing beyond design conditions.

2 Corrosion or erosion from process media outside material compatibility.

3 Seat leakage due to dirt, debris, or lack of filtration.

4 Damage from overtightening or incorrect actuator sizing or setup.
------------------------------
### Needle Type Valve
Inclusions:
1Precision control performance under design conditions.

2 Sealing and shut-off integrity at rated pressure.

3 Structural integrity of needle, seat, and stem.
Exclusions:
1 Blockage due to particulate matter or poor system cleanliness.

2 Damage caused by over-torquing or forcing the needle.

3 Leakage due to wear from high-cycle or pulsating service not specified in design.
------------------------------
### Ball Type Valve
Inclusions:
1 Leakage protection due to faulty sealing material under normal operating conditions.

2 Corrosion protection integrity (if coated by manufacturer).

3 Repair/replacement of defective internal components during warranty period..
Exclusions:
1  Damage from improper installation, misalignment, or overtightening.

2 Normal wear and tear of PTFE seats, seals, and packing.

3 Failure due to operation outside design pressure/temperature.

4 Damage from abrasive or corrosive media not specified for the valve.

5Tampering, unauthorized modifications, or non-OEM spare usage.

6 External surface/cosmetic damage due to mishandling.
------------------------------
