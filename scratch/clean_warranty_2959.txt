### Solenoid Type Valve
Inclusions:
1 Warranty covers manufacturing defects in coil, body, and sealing components.

2 Covers electrical faults in solenoid coil (burnout due to normal usage).

3 Replacement/repair of defective valve within warranty period under normal operating conditions.

4 Assurance against leakage caused by faulty assembly or defective sealing.
Exclusions:
1 Damage due to improper wiring, incorrect voltage supply, or power surges.

2 Failure caused by corrosive/abrasive media not specified in product datasheet.

3 Wear and tear of seals and gaskets due to normal use.

4 Damage due to improper installation, dry operation, or exposure to incompatible chemicals.

### Motorized Type Valve
Inclusions:
1 Coverage for defects in motor, actuator, and valve body.

2 Warranty includes malfunction due to faulty motor windings, gear assembly, or actuator electronics.

3 Assurance against leakage under specified pressure and temperature conditions.

4 Repair or replacement of defective unit within warranty duration.
Exclusions:
1 Damage caused by overloading, incorrect torque settings, or exceeding duty cycles.

2 Burnout due to unstable or incorrect power supply.

3 Wear and tear of mechanical moving parts (e.g., gears, couplings) from normal operation.

4 Corrosion or scaling caused by aggressive or incompatible media.
------------------------------
### Pneumatic Actuated Type Valve
Inclusions:
1 Warranty covers actuator cylinder, piston, and valve body defects.

2 Coverage for faulty actuator seals and springs under normal use.

3Assurance against leakage due to defective diaphragm/sealing.

4 Replacement/repair of valve if pneumatic actuation fails due to manufacturing defect.
Exclusions:
1 Failure caused by contaminated air supply (dust, moisture, oil).

2 Damage due to exceeding rated pressure limits.

3 Wear of seals and diaphragms from regular use.

4 Incorrect installation (improper air line connection, inadequate lubrication)..
------------------------------
### Diaphragm Type Valve
Inclusions:
1 Coverage against defects in diaphragm material and valve body.

2 Manufacturing faults in actuator or control components.

3 Protection against leakage caused by improper bonding/sealing.

4 Replacement/repair of defective parts under rated conditions.
Exclusions:
1 Normal wear and tear of diaphragms.

2 Chemical degradation of diaphragm due to incompatible media.

3 Damage caused by exceeding rated pressure or temperature.

4 Faults arising from improper maintenance, cleaning with harsh chemicals, or dry operation.
