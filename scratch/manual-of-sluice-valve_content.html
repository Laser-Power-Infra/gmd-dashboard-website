Manual Of Sluice Valve 1. Transportation And Storage

1.1 Transportation

-Transport valves in the closed position to protect sealing surfaces.
- Use appropriate lifting equipment; do not lift the valve by the handwheel or stem.
- Avoid impact or dropping to prevent damage to valve components.

1.2 Storage

- Store in a clean, dry environment away from direct sunlight and corrosive chemicals.
- Cover the valve openings to prevent ingress of dust and debris.
- Periodically inspect and rotate valve stem to ensure functionality if stored longterm.

2. Installation

2.1 Pre-Installation Checks

- Verify the valve type, size, pressure rating, and material against service requirements.
- Inspect the valve for damage during transit or storage.
- Ensure piping is clean and aligned to prevent stress on valve body.

2.2 Installation Guidelines

- Install the valve in the correct flow direction as indicated by the arrow on the body.
- Ensure valves are fully closed before installation.
- Support the valve to avoid unnecessary pipe strain.
- Use appropriate gaskets and tighten bolts in a crisscross pattern evenly.
- Allow sufficient space for handwheel or actuator operation and maintenance access.

3. Troubleshooting

Issue: Valve leaking when closed

- Possible Cause: Worn or damaged seat or gate.
- Solution: Inspect sealing surfaces; repair or replace parts as needed.

Issue: Valve difficult to operate

- Possible Cause: Corrosion or debris on stem/gate.
- Solution: Clean components; lubricate stem; check for actuator faults.

Issue: Leakage at bonnet or flanges

- Possible Cause: Gasket failure or loose bolts.
- Solution: Replace gasket; tighten or replace bolt.

Issue: No flow despite valve being open

- Possible Cause: Gate jammed or disconnected stem.
- Solution: Inspect internals; check stem connection and gate movement.

4. Maintenance

4.1 Periodic Inspection

- Check valve operation regularly by fully opening and closing.
- Look for signs of leakage, corrosion, or mechanical wear.
- Inspect bolts, gaskets, and paint coating.

4.2 Routine Maintenance

Type of Inspection Recommended Frequency Visual Inspection Monthly Operational Check Quarterly (every 3 months) Full Functional Test Annually (1x per year) Lubrication/Servicing Annually Leakage Test Annually

4.3 Overhaul Procedure

- Isolate valve from system pressure before disassembly.
- Remove bonnet to access internal components.
- Inspect and replace worn parts such as gate, seat, stem, and seals.
- Reassemble using correct torque specifications and pressure test before reinstallation.

5. Safety Production

- Always isolate and depressurize pipeline before working on the valve.
- Use proper lifting techniques and PPE.
- Confirm absence of hazardous fluids before disassembly.
