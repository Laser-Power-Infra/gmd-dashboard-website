Manual Of Butterfly Valves
1. Transportation and Storage
1.1 Transportation
- Handle the valve carefully to prevent damage to the body, disc, or actuator.
- Use lifting lugs or slings to lift large valves. Do not lift by actuator or handwheel.
- Keep valve in the closed or slightly open position during transport to protect the sealing surfaces.

Manual Of Non-Return Valve
1. Introduction
A Non-Return Valve (NRV), also known as a check valve, allows fluid to flow in one
direction only and prevents backflow. This manual provides guidance on handling,
installation, operation, troubleshooting, and maintenance.
2.Transportation and Storage
2.1 Transportation
 Always transport NRVs in their original packaging.
 Avoid dropping or exposing the valve to impact.
 Secure during transportation to prevent movement or vibration

Manual Of Dual Plate
1. Introduction
A Dual Plate Check Valve (also called a double door or wafer check valve) is designed to \
prevent backflow in piping systems. It uses two spring-loaded plates that open with forward \
flow and close quickly to prevent reverse flow, minimizing water hammer. This manual \
outlines best practices for the valve’s lifecycle, including handling, installation, and \
maintenance.\
