### Cryogenic Type Valve
Inclusions:
1 Warranty covers manufacturing defects in valve body, bonnet, stem, and seats.

2 Coverage against leakage under rated cryogenic temperatures and pressures.

3 Functional defects in extended bonnet or special low-temperature seals.

4 Repair/replacement of defective parts due to faulty material or assembly..
Exclusions:
1 Damage due to improper handling, rapid temperature cycling, or incorrect insulation.

2 Seal/seat wear caused by repeated thermal contraction/expansion (normal use).

3 Corrosion or material degradation due to incompatible cryogenic fluids.

4 Failure caused by ice formation or lack of specified purging/maintenance.
------------------------------
### Safety Type Valve
Inclusions:
1 Warranty covers defects in spring, lever, or safety mechanism.

2 Functional failure to open/close at calibrated safety set points due to manufacturing defects.

3 Coverage against leakage caused by faulty sealing surfaces.

4 Repair or replacement of valve if failure occurs under recommended operating limits.
Exclusions:
1Tampering with factory set calibration or unauthorized adjustments.

2 Damage from exposure to aggressive chemicals or high-velocity particulate media.

3 Wear of seats, seals, or discs due to repeated actuation cycles.

4 Improper storage, handling, or installation (e.g., overtightening, incorrect alignment)..
------------------------------
### Four Way Type Valve
Inclusions:
1 Warranty covers manufacturing defects in body, rotor, and internal sealing components.

2 Functional failure to divert/redirect flow as per design due to defective mechanism.

3 Coverage for leakage at rated pressure and temperature conditions.

4 Repair/replacement of faulty unit under normal service operation.
Exclusions:
1 Damage caused by excessive torque, incorrect actuator fitment, or forced operation.

2 Wear and tear of sealing surfaces or gaskets from regular switching.

3 Corrosion, scaling, or erosion caused by incompatible process fluids.

4 Improper mounting or misalignment during installation.
