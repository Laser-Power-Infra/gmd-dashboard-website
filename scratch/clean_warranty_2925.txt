### Tamper Proof Valve
Inclusions:
1. Coverage against manufacturing defects in valve body, float, sealing components, and tamper-proof locking system

2. Protection against leakage or malfunction caused by material faults under normal operating pressure

3. Integrity of corrosion-resistant coatings (if factory-applied)

4. Free repair or replacement of defective parts within warranty period
Exclusions:
1. Damage due to improper installation or overtightening

2. Failure caused by overpressure, water hammer, or surge events

3. Normal wear and tear of seals, gaskets, and seats

4. Unauthorized modification, tampering, or dismantling of valve

5. Damage from debris, corrosive chemicals, or water quality outside design parameters

6. External coating damage due to mishandling or environmental exposure beyond specified limits
